VFX picker

Model picker
Free Creator Store textures (decals) and VFX model packs, grouped by the role they would play. Click up to 3 per role; the click order is the rank (click again to drop one). A flipbook is a grid sheet of frames (ParticleEmitter.FlipbookLayout): the guess comes from the name, so check the thumbnail and set the grid on each pick. Model packs with scripts are never run: their scripts are deleted when a pick is vetted in Studio. Export picks saves vfx-picks.json (usually to Downloads).
